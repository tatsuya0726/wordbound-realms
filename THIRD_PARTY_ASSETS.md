## RYU ITO: Forgive

Runtime file: `assets/bgm/forgive.mp3`. `music.js` credits the title, composer RYU ITO, and https://ryu110.com/.

Official sources checked 2026-11-06:
- Track: https://ryu110.com/forgive/
- Terms: https://ryu110.com/rules/

The track page offers free BGM subject to the terms. The terms allow listed uses without charge, require attribution, and prohibit unauthorized music-file sales and music streaming. The non-YouTube attribution example identifies RYU ITO and the homepage; the game currently displays both.

Neither page expressly addresses free or paid browser games or embedded audio-file redistribution. The terms direct other or uncertain uses to the author.
